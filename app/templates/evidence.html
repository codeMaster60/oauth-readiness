How the score works

The model never picks the number. It pulls facts out of the docs, and these rules turn them into a 1 to 5. Same facts, same score, every time.

Base1 point (create the app)
Provider review before real users / scale+1
Review can take 10+ days+1 (and +0.5 more if 30+ days)
Third-party security assessment+1
Business / domain / page verification+0.5
Partner-gated or discretionary access+2
Paid plan or fee required+1
Install cap until reviewed+0.5

Rounded, clamped to 1–5. Words: {% for k, l in labels.items() %}{{ k }} {{ l }}{% if not loop.last %} · {% endif %}{% endfor %}. The second half of the label comes from the wait itself: minutes, same day, days, weeks, or "approval not guaranteed" when access is at the provider's discretion. That way the headline can't say "same day" next to a week-long review.
