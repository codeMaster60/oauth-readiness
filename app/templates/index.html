1 · Your OAuth app

You register one app with the provider. Google, Slack, whoever. You get a client ID, a secret, and a callback URL. It's the name your users see on the "wants access to your account" screen.

2 · Your integration

In Composio, that app becomes an auth config. Until you have your own, Composio's managed app fills in. Fine for building. Your users just see "Composio" instead of you.

3 · Each user's connection

Every user who clicks Allow gets a connection tied to that app. Switch apps later and everyone reconnects. Worth deciding early.
