1 · Your OAuth app

You register one app with the provider (Google, Slack…). It gets a client ID and secret and a callback URL. It's what users see on the “App wants access” screen.

2 · Your integration

With Composio, that app becomes an auth config. Until you bring your own, Composio's managed app stands in — great for building, but users see “Composio”.

3 · Each user's connection

Every user who clicks “Allow” creates a connected account tied to that app. Switch apps later and they must all reconnect — so pick your path early.
